# Customer Offboarding

## Question

How does customer offboarding works?

## Answer

We will get a work order from CD team "Customer Offboarding until DD.MM.YYYY". In such a case, there are certain tasks to do, depending on the location of the cluster. This process is described in [IPSSupPrc02 - Customer Offboarding](https://exasol.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/SUPPORT/pages/6754846/IPSSupPrc02+-+Customer+Offboarding).
