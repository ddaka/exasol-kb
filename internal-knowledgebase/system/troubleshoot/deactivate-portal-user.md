# How to deactivate Portal Users

It may be the case that a customer requests the deactivation of individual user logins for their account, or that all user logins for an account need to be deactivated as part of the customer offboarding process. If this is the case, please follow this procedure.

## Identify Contact

First, identify the contact you need to deactivate by selecting “Contacts” from the “Related List Quick Links” menu in the upper right corner of the account page.

Search for the contact you want to deactivate and check whether the “Is Active Portal User” checkbox is selected. If the checkbox is not selected, no further action is required because the contact does not have a portal user. If it is selected, click on the contact name.

![Contact list with active portal users](images/disable_portal_user_contact_list.png)

## Deactivate Portal User

To disable portal access, select “Disable Customer User” from the drop-down menu in the upper right corner of the screen.

![Contact dropdown menu](images/disable_portal_user_dropdown.png)

A popup window will now ask for confirmation. Please confirm by clicking “Disable Customer User”.

![Disable Customer User popup](images/disable_portal_user_popup.png)

The portal user has now been successfully disabled.
