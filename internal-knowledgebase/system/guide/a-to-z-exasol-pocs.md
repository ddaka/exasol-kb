# A to Z: Exasol POCs

## Purpose

Provide a practical runbook for handling Exasol POC requests consistently.

## Standard workflow

1. Sales opens support ticket and links POC context.
1. Ticket is assigned and ownership is confirmed.
1. Collect required customer environment information.
1. Perform sizing.
1. Prepare implementation plan and customer session.
1. Execute installation and hand over to sales/customer.
1. Document work, log time, and close ticket.
1. Communicate POC status internally.

## Intake checklist

- Deployment model (on-prem/cloud, v7/v8, containerized/classic).
- Node count and sizing targets (CPU/RAM/storage).
- Network requirements and access constraints.
- Security/compliance constraints.
- Customer timeline and success criteria.

## Delivery preparation

- Confirm image/package readiness.
- Ensure customer-side prerequisites are completed.
- Prepare implementation step list with time estimates.
- Align meeting agenda with sales and customer.

## Execution expectations

- Follow planned steps; capture deviations.
- Validate cluster health and core connectivity.
- Hand over to sales engineer for workload/demo phase.

## Post-install closure

- Document cluster specs and key actions in ticket.
- Record irregularities, risks, and follow-up items.
- Log spent time.
- Close ticket and send internal status update.
